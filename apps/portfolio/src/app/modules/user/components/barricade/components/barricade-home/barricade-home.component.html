Match Mechanics Recap

  • 2-Player: Reach the opposite board edge row (Row 9 vs Row 1).
  • 4-Player: Race to touch the exact center square (e5).
  • Barricades: Walls span 2 grooves. Can never completely seal off a player's path to their goal.
  • Actions: Move pawn up to allocated steps and place up to allocated walls per turn.
