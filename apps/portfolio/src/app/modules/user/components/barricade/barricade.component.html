Gameplay Rules & Mechanics

Summit Castle

The first player to land a token exactly on the summit castle node claims ultimate victory!

Barricade Obstacles

Barricades block paths. You cannot leap through them, but landing on one captures and relocates it anywhere!

Opponent Capture

Landing directly on an opponent piece sends their token all the way back to their home starting camp.

Exact Dice Movement

Tokens move the exact value rolled on the die (1–6). BFS pathfinding guides legal directions with zero backtracking.
