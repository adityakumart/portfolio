Tactical Board Game

Barricade (Malefiz)

Scale the pyramid, bypass bottlenecks, capture barricades, and knock opponents back to base camp to claim the summit castle!

Gameplay Rules & Mechanics

Summit Castle

The first player to land a token exactly on the summit castle node wins the game!

11 Barricades

Barricades block paths. You cannot leap through them, but landing on one captures and relocates it!

Opponent Capture

Landing directly on an opponent piece sends their token back to their home starting base camp.

Exact Dice Movement

Tokens move the exact value rolled on the die (1–6). Breadth-First pathfinding prevents backtracking.
