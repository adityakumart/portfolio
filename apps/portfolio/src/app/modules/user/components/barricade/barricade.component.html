Barricade Quoridor 9x9

Navigate your pawn to the opposite row or place barricades to obstruct your opponent.

Tactical Rules & Mechanics

Goal Row

Player 1 (Red) wins upon reaching any square on row 9. Computer (Blue) wins upon reaching row 1.

10 Barricades

Each player has 10 walls. Place walls horizontally or vertically across 2 cells to obstruct your opponent.

No Trap Rule

Fair Play: A wall can never be placed if it completely seals off either player from reaching their goal.

Pawn Jumps

When facing an adjacent opponent, leap directly over them or jump diagonally if a wall is behind them.
